What the agent does

  1. Reads this site, the skills library and the data repository.
  2. Greets you and asks what you want to do, from a numbered menu.
  3. States a plan and loads the right skills and data.
  4. Stops for your judgment at every checkpoint.
  5. Hands back the finished deliverable. You decide.
