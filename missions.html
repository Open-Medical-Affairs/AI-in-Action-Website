Team missions · 30 minutes each

01

Field Medical · 30 minutes

🧑‍⚕️Prepare me for a difficult conversation

In three days you have a 30-minute scientific exchange with Dr Adaeze Okafor. She has been publicly sceptical about your compound and she knows this field better than you do. Walk in ready.

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

A brief someone could actually absorb in the ten minutes before the meeting. Not a dossier — a brief. It should tell you who she is, what she genuinely cares about, what has changed since you last spoke, questions worth asking her, the questions she is likely to ask you, and the evidence to have ready.

What you have

  • kol-dossiers.mdbackground and publication record for three experts
  • interaction-notes.mdwhat happened last time
  • product-profile.mdthe compound and its evidence
  • evidence-landscape.mdwhat is known in this disease
  • medical-plan.mdthe current scientific priorities

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/kol-engagement-brief.md. how long should a brief be? What must never appear in one? What does a good question to a KOL look like, and what does a bad one look like?

02

Field Insights · 30 minutes

📋Find what humans would miss

Here are a quarter's worth of field interaction records. Tell leadership what they need to know.

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

Not a list of themes with counts. Themes are what a spreadsheet gives you. You want insights: what is happening, why it matters, what it changes, and what someone should do about it — with a name against each action. Plus the three things underneath that usually get lost: where sources genuinely disagree, the weak signals only one or two people mentioned, and what the field conspicuously did not say.

What you have

  • field-observations.csvthe records themselves
  • medical-plan.mdthe current scientific priorities
  • evidence-landscape.mdwhat is known
  • product-profile.mdthe compound

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/field-insight-synthesis.md. what makes an observation an insight? How should confidence be stated? What must never be smoothed over?

03

Congress Intelligence · 30 minutes

🏛️The congress just ended. Tell leadership what changed.

Three days of data, sixty presentations, two competitor announcements. Leadership has four minutes. What changed, and what should we do about it?

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

Four sections, one page: What changed · Why it matters · What to watch · What we should do Not a summary of what was presented. A summary is what everyone else will produce, and by the time it lands people will have formed their impressions already.

What you have

  • congress-abstracts.mdwhat was presented
  • competitor-announcements.mdpress releases and corporate statements
  • evidence-landscape.mdwhat was known going in
  • medical-plan.mdour current position and narrative
  • product-profile.mdour compound

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/congress-intelligence.md. how fast must this land? How long can it be? What may and may not be cited from a congress abstract?

04

Scientific Communications · 30 minutes

📚What should we be publishing, and what should we stop?

Determine what our scientific communication strategy should be over the next 12 months.

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

A strategy, not a list of papers sequenced by when data arrive. It should work backwards from what each audience is trying to decide, show where we are over-communicating and where we are silent, and separate two things that are constantly confused: - an evidence gap — nobody has answered the question. Fix: research. - a communication gap — it has been answered and nobody heard. Fix: publish.

What you have

  • publication-plan.mdwhat is currently planned
  • evidence-landscape.mdour evidence and the field's
  • field-observations.csvwhat clinicians are actually asking
  • medical-plan.mdthe scientific priorities
  • product-profile.mdthe compound

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/scientific-communication-strategy.md. what does your organisation require before something is published? What happens to negative results? What is a realistic timeline for influencing a guideline?

05

Medical Strategy · 30 minutes

🧠Build next year's scientific priorities

Develop the scientific priorities for next year's medical plan — and be prepared to defend why these and not others.

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

Three to five priorities. Not twelve. Each with the outcome it seeks, why it outranks the one below it, and what we give up to pursue it. Activities traced to priorities. An explicit list of what we are deliberately not doing. And a success measure for each that could genuinely come back negative.

What you have

  • medical-plan.mdthis year's plan
  • evidence-landscape.mdwhere the evidence stands
  • field-observations.csvwhat the field is telling us
  • competitor-announcements.mdwhat changed competitively
  • publication-plan.mdwhat is planned
  • product-profile.mdthe compound

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/medical-strategy-plan.md. what must every activity trace to? What counts as a real measure of success in your organisation?

06

Evidence Generation · 30 minutes

🔬Tell us what we still don't know

Identify what we don't know, decide which gaps are worth closing, and allocate a £5 million evidence budget.

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

A ranked set of gaps, each with the decision it blocks and whose decision it is, the study design that would close it, and what it costs. Then an allocation against the £5m — with an explicit line, and everything below it, and what the organisation loses by not funding those.

What you have

  • evidence-landscape.mdwhat exists
  • product-profile.mdthe compound and its evidence base
  • field-observations.csvthe questions clinicians actually ask
  • medical-plan.mdthe scientific priorities
  • publication-plan.mdwhat is planned

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/evidence-gap-analysis.md. what does your organisation require before it funds a study? What makes evidence credible to the people you need to convince?

07

Launch Planning Swarm · 45 minutes. Run Wave 1, stop at Gate 1 and approve (or reject) the platform

🚀Build the whole medical launch plan — with a team of digital workers

You are the Launch Lead for ADIPOSYN's proposed label expansion (or DERMALYX, or NORVANTIB). Build the complete Medical Affairs launch plan by running an org chart of digital workers — and hand a human the final verdict, even if that verdict is "not ready".

What “done” looks like, inputs and Round 2

1. A one-page launch facts sheet — with the indication wording marked as an assumption, because nobody gave it to you. 2. The six-month question list: answered / answerable / unanswerable. 3. The org chart: which digital workers you started, which you did not, and the context packet each one received. 4. Workstream plans, integrated: the joins checked — label dependency, citable before discussed, narrative consistency, owner capacity. 5. A critical path and gate criteria written *before* the gate. 6. An independent readiness verdict and a gap register where every gap has a person and a date.

What you have

  • product-profile.mdthe compound and its evidence
  • launch-readiness-register.mdwhere the steering committee thinks it is

When it finishes, push on it

Round 2 · teach it your house rules

Your file is house-rules/medical-launch-plan.md.
