Missions/thirty-day-capstone
Level 3 · Team13 skills

Run the next 30 days of Medical Affairs

What it does. Prepare a coordinated 30-day draft plan for the selected fictional product. Prioritize evidence, field actions and content within the supplied constraints. Deliver a leadership brief, action tracker and scientific briefing, with source dependencies so new evidence can update affected work.

1Paste this into Grok Bot

Pick the practice data, then copy.

2What you need

  • Grok Bot, or any agent that can read GitHub (set-up).
  • Nothing to download: the agent fetches the synthetic files itself (data).
  • About 30 minutes.

Files it reads (8):

  • medical-plan.md
  • product-profile.md
  • field-observations.csv
  • evidence-landscape.md
  • integrated-evidence-plan.csv
  • launch-readiness-register.md
  • content_assets.csv
  • projects.csv

3The steps

  1. Paste the prompt into Grok Bot (or any agent). It loads medical-affairs-foundations and the lead skill medical-strategy-plan.
  2. It scans the data for possible safety findings first and tells you about any before doing anything else.
  3. medical-strategy-plan does the core job: Build or challenge a medical plan — the scientific priorities, the choices behind them, and the activities that trace to them.
  4. It hands off to field-insight-synthesis: Turn a body of field medical observations into insights leadership can act on.
  5. It hands off to integrated-evidence-plan: Build the cross-functional evidence plan for an asset — every study, analysis and publication across clinical development, Medical Affairs, HEOR and market access, sequenced against the decisions and milestones each has to serve.
  6. It hands off to medical-content-operations: Plan and maintain non-promotional Medical Affairs content across channels using audience needs, evidence, content inventories and engagement measures.
  7. It hands off to executive-briefing: Distil any body of Medical Affairs work into the two-page brief or ten-slide read a leadership team actually absorbs — situation, what changed, what it means, what to decide, by when.
  8. It hands off to medical-slide-deck: Build non-promotional medical slide decks — advisory board decks, MSL scientific presentations, congress readouts, medical-to-medical (M2M) presentations, internal data reviews and training material.
  9. deliverable-quality-review checks the draft independently and the agent fixes what it finds.
  10. It hands you the deliverables as designed files, marked as drafts. You review and decide.

4Where you decide

  • Safety scan first Scan human-sourced records for possible safety, PQC or special-situation findings before analysis (AGENTS.md).
  • You decide Outputs are drafts for qualified human review; the human is the final judge (AGENTS.md).

You get

  • Leadership brief
  • 30-day action tracker
  • Scientific briefing
  • Source dependencies and change log
