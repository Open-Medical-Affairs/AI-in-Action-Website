What it does. Design an accessible listening and co-creation activity for the selected therapeutic area. Link patient priorities to decisions and respect quotation permissions.

Pick the practice data, then copy.

2What you need

  • Grok Bot, or any agent that can read GitHub (set-up).
  • Nothing to download: the agent fetches the synthetic files itself (data).
  • About 15–20 minutes.

Files it reads (4):

  • patient_partnerships.csv
  • plain-language-source.md
  • medical-education-needs.md
  • evidence-landscape.md

3The steps

  1. Paste the prompt into Grok Bot (or any agent). It loads medical-affairs-foundations and the lead skill patient-engagement-planning.
  2. It scans the data for possible safety findings first and tells you about any before doing anything else.
  3. patient-engagement-planning does the core task: Design Medical Affairs partnerships and co-creation with patients, caregivers and patient organizations around scientific needs.
  4. It hands off to plain-language-summary: Write plain language summaries of clinical research for patients and the public — trial results lay summaries, plain language summaries of publications, and patient-facing scientific material.
  5. deliverable-quality-review checks the draft independently and the agent fixes what it finds.
  6. It hands you the deliverables as designed files, marked as drafts. You review and decide.

4Where you decide

  • Safety scan first Scan human-sourced records for possible safety, PQC or special-situation findings before analysis (AGENTS.md).
  • You decide Outputs are drafts for qualified human review; the human is the final judge (AGENTS.md).

You get

  • Accessible participation plan
  • Discussion guide
  • Feedback-to-decision table
