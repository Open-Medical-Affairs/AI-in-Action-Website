Missions/launch-plan-swarm
Level 4 · Swarm34 skills

Build the whole medical launch plan

What it does. Act as the Launch Lead for the selected fictional product's proposed label expansion. Build the launch facts sheet and six-month question list, staff an org chart of digital workers (one per launch workstream, each with its own context packet), run them in waves with human decision gates, integrate the joins, and finish with an independent readiness verdict that is allowed to be 'not ready'. Every gap carries a named human owner and a date.

1Paste this into Grok Bot

Pick the practice data, then copy.

2What you need

  • Grok Bot, or any agent that can read GitHub (set-up).
  • Nothing to download: the agent fetches the synthetic files itself (data).
  • About 45 minutes.

3The steps

  1. Paste the prompt into Grok Bot (or any agent). It loads medical-affairs-foundations and the lead skill medical-launch-plan.
  2. It scans the data for possible safety findings first and tells you about any before doing anything else.
  3. Wave 0: Launch Lead. Facts sheet, six-month question list, worker roster.
  4. Wave 1: Strategy worker, Evidence worker, Narrative worker, Value & Access worker, Guideline Scout. These set the objectives, the evidence position and the words everyone else uses.
  5. Gate 1 (you decide): strategy, platform and evidence position. Nothing downstream should be built on an unapproved narrative.
  6. Wave 2: Pubs & Congress worker, Field Medical worker, Field Training worker, Expert Input worker, Education worker, Patient Partnership worker, Medical Information worker, Safety Liaison, Content & MLR worker. Each consumes the approved platform and the question list.
  7. Wave 3: Launch PMO, Metrics Analyst. Integrate dates, dependencies, owners and measures across everything above.
  8. Wave 4: Readiness Auditor. Independent challenge and a verdict.
  9. Gate 2 (you decide): integrated plan and readiness verdict. Accept, send back, or accept with named gaps.
  10. It hands you the deliverables as designed files, marked as drafts. You review and decide.

4Where you decide

  • Safety scan first Scan human-sourced records for possible safety, PQC or special-situation findings before analysis (AGENTS.md).
  • Gate 1 Human: strategy, platform and evidence position: Nothing downstream should be built on an unapproved narrative
  • Gate 2 Human: integrated plan and readiness verdict: Accept, send back, or accept with named gaps
  • You decide Outputs are drafts for qualified human review; the human is the final judge (AGENTS.md).

You get

  • Integrated launch plan with org chart and workstream plans
  • Critical path, gate criteria and RACI
  • Six-month question list with routes
  • Independent readiness verdict and gap register
  • Leadership deck
