Get the data

Copy a link. Your agent fetches it.

Nothing needs to land on your laptop. Copy a direct download link (or a ready-to-paste instruction) and give it to Grok Bot or any agent: it downloads the file onto its own machine. Synthetic files are fictional and safe to practise on. Public sources are real: we copy the official link and show the licence, and Link only sources must be used at the source, never copied.
